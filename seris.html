MEET SERIS

Five members.
One group.
A new era.

SERIS is an international virtual K-pop girl group that debuted on July 30, 2026. The current lineup consists of five members: Mirae, Ara, Irin, Yuna and Sora.

Through pop, rap, melodies and distinct vocal identities, SERIS creates a musical and visual world of its own. The group’s songs are mainly in English and Korean, exploring a range of styles and moods.

Originally a six-member group, SERIS has experienced changes along the way, including Yuna’s hiatus and Nari’s departure. In 2026, the group released NEW ERA, a five-track EP. Its official fandom is called Seraphime.

OUR STORY

From six to five

30 JUL 2026

Debut

SERIS debuted as a six-member group: Mirae, Ara, Irin, Yuna, Sora and Nari.

2026

A time of change

Yuna took a hiatus from group activities. Later, Nari Villanueva, the group’s original maknae, left the project. SERIS continued with five members.

OCT 2026

Yuna’s return

Yuna’s return was announced after the release of NEW ERA. She had also taken part in the EP.

SERIS · EP · 2026

NEW ERA

NEW ERA is a five-track EP by SERIS, released in 2026. Its sound is shaped by a blend of 2-step and Jersey influences, first explored in DISCO QUEEN.

  1. NEW ERA
  2. DISCO QUEEN
  3. I SEE IT
  4. SEASIDE
  5. TEN MISSED CALLS

DISCO QUEEN

DISCO QUEEN was the first song Veyra wrote for NEW ERA. It was also her first track to explore a blend of 2-step and Jersey sounds, a style that went on to shape the whole EP.

SEASIDE

Written by the creator of SERIS, SEASIDE tells the story of a carefree summer day by the sea, with a playful and slightly silly atmosphere.

SOLO RELEASE

YOUR ANGEL

YOUR ANGEL is Ara’s solo song, centered on the feeling of being cherished and adored. Through images of flowers, clouds and light, it presents a soft, dreamy world where Ara embraces the love and attention around her.
