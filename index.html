01 / music

Who am I?

✧

Veyra is an artist and songwriter who creates music through a combination of ideas, experimentation and digital tools. Her work moves between different sounds and styles, with a particular connection to dreamy, nostalgic and ethereal atmospheres.

The music

Veyra’s music is a space for exploring stories, emotions and ideas. Each song can have its own identity: some feel delicate and dreamlike, while others explore darker or more unusual moods.

Rather than staying within a single genre, Veyra enjoys experimenting with different sounds and finding the atmosphere that feels right for each song. Dreamcore and pop are part of her creative world, alongside other influences.

The creative process

Every song begins with an idea. It might be a theme, an emotion, a story or a few words that suggest a direction. From there, the idea develops through writing, experimentation and different versions of the music.

Veyra uses platforms including Treblo and Suno as part of her creative process. They offer different ways to explore sounds and develop ideas, while each project can take its own path from the first concept to the finished song.

What music means to her

She has always dreamed of sharing her music with the world. After joining Suno, she was influenced by many artists who helped her discover her own artistic identity. She always starts with lyrics she writes herself, then builds the songs around them.

My World

Inspiration

My inspiration comes from personal experiences, simple phrases or even a single word. Sometimes, an idea can also come from an image, like it did with WAKE UP.

Other artists inspire me every day too. Discovering their music and receiving their support has helped me explore new ideas and keep developing my own style.

Atmosphere

Dreamcore can be difficult to explain to someone who isn’t familiar with it. But to me, it can be found in many places. The same goes for jungle: elements of both can appear in K-pop, modern pop and J-pop.

I feel a strong connection between K-pop and dreamy sounds. I wasn’t always a big K-pop fan: I knew the main groups, but it was only when I started exploring further that I discovered more of that world. Now, dreamcore feels like a part of who I am.

From idea to song

It’s hard to explain exactly how my ideas begin. I often have a different song in my head each day, and my lyrics can start with a phrase, a word or a passion I want to share.

Once I know what I want to express, I write the lyrics and build the song around them.

The music of Veyra

Veyra’s music brings together different ideas, stories and sounds. Her songs explore a range of atmospheres, from dreamy and nostalgic to darker and more unusual moods. Among her releases on Suno are WAKE UP, alone, angelic childhood and dream.

angelic childhood was created in collaboration with vhaeli, a popular Suno artist who has reached 30,000 followers. After discovering Veyra’s profile, vhaeli invited her to work together. They shared the work on the lyrics and both contributed to the production.

Combining dreamy and jungle sounds, the song explores the wish to remain a child and the nostalgia that comes with growing up. It also reflects on how memories can slowly fade over time, even when we want to hold on to them.

Across her music, Veyra enjoys exploring different styles and giving each song its own atmosphere. Through writing and experimentation, she turns ideas into music and shares them with her listeners.
